GRAMMAR · 1/2

Le passif (lecture)

Reading passive sentences
Est / sont + past participle (+ par) shows what is done to the subject. You mainly need to recognise it.
EXAMPLES
Le courrier est distribué par la poste.
The mail is delivered by the post office.
Les rues sont nettoyées chaque matin.
The streets are cleaned every morning.

GRAMMAR · 2/2

Quelqu'un, quelque chose, tout, chacun

Indefinite words
These words talk about people or things without naming them: someone, something, everything, each one.
EXAMPLES
Quelqu'un a téléphoné.
Someone called.
Chacun paie sa part.
Each one pays their share.

About Élan 🦉

A French course that follows the classroom method — in your pocket.

Élan is a French-learning app built around one idea: the way French is taught in good classrooms works, and an app should follow it instead of replacing it with games and streaks. Élan teaches French from A1 to C2 through dossiers — small, complete lessons in the style of the Édito coursebooks used in language schools worldwide.

The method

Every dossier opens with real material: dialogues and texts in natural, everyday French, with listening pieces of around two minutes in real voices. You work through comprehension first, then the vocabulary from those documents, then the grammar in three beats — warm-up, how it works, practice — then exercises, and you finish by producing French yourself, in writing and out loud.

Who it is for

Élan is for learners who want real French: people preparing for life, work, study or exams in French, and anyone who wants a course that treats them like a student. A placement test across the four skills puts you at your honest level before you start, and your progress follows you between your phone and your computer.
